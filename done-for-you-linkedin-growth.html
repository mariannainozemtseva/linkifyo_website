How it works

  1. step_01 · week 1

    Strategy and profile

    A kickoff call on your goals and the people you need to reach. I define your ideal client profile, rewrite your profile and build a content strategy around your business goals.

  2. step_02 · ongoing

    Posts in your voice

    One to four short calls a month to collect your news and expertise. I write, you review and approve, I publish. Up to 8 or 12 posts a month, depending on the plan.

  3. step_03 · ongoing

    The right people in your network

    I regularly add people who match your ideal client profile, so your posts reach decision-makers. On Growth + Networking I also handle incoming comments, requests and messages.

  4. step_04 · monthly

    Analytics and adjustments

    A monthly report on what worked and what didn’t, with suggestions. Every quarter, a deep-dive to adjust the strategy to your business.

  5. step_05 · result

    Inbound from the right people

    Clients, partners and talent know your name before they ever meet you, and they reach out first. Plus warm intros from my network, when relevant.
